# 10. Random Trait
Roll **1d10** after generating the item's type, function, originator, and mechanics.
1. **Cursed**
2. **Anatomical Oddity**, roll 1d20
3. **Strange Property**, roll 1d20
4. **Material / Surface**, roll 1d20
5. **Form / Decoration**, roll 1d20
6. **Physical Transformation**, roll 1d20
7. **Elemental / Environmental Theme**, roll 1d20
8. **Supernatural Theme**, roll 1d20
9. **Creature / Nature Theme**, roll 1d20
10. **Exceptional**, choose Legendary or Sentient

---
## 1. Cursed
The item gains a meaningful drawback connected to its existing function, originator, or mechanics.
Possible examples:
- Using it costs HP
- Using it causes exhaustion
- It cannot be willingly discarded
- Its power sometimes affects the wielder too
- It attracts hostile creatures
- It demands some recurring action
- Its beneficial effect comes with an equivalent penalty
- Its summoned creatures become hostile afterward
- It requires increasingly costly sacrifices
- It twists its function under certain circumstances
- Removing it requires _Remove Curse_
- It gradually alters its wielder
This one works better as a designed curse rather than another generic table.

# 10. Exceptional
Choose one:
## Legendary
The item becomes **Legendary**.
Its existing abilities should be strengthened, expanded, or made more significant enough to justify Legendary rarity.
## Sentient
The item becomes **Sentient**.
Determine or choose:
- Intelligence level
- Personality
- Alignment or general disposition, if relevant
- Communication method
- Wants
- Fears
- Relationship to its creator or originator
Sentience does not automatically mean the item is helpful.
