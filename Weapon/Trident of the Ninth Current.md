The Trident of the Ninth Current was made by Triton tidekeepers who used enchanted weapons as working tools as often as instruments of war. Its three dark metal tines curve slightly inward, and seawater constantly beads across their surface even when the trident has been dry for days. When submerged, faint blue lines appear along the shaft like branching currents on a chart.

The trident's primary enchantment reproduces **Control Water**. By planting the butt of the weapon against the ground, pointing its head toward a body of water, and willing the current to change, the bearer can expend **2 charges** to cast the spell. The trident holds **5 charges** and regains **1d4 + 1 charges each dawn**, provided it was submerged in natural water at some point during the previous day. Triton tidekeepers originally used the weapon to clear dangerous channels, redirect sudden currents, expose submerged structures, and protect settlements during violent changes in the sea. Its use in battle came naturally later, since the same magic that could open a safe route for a vessel could also tear one apart.

The weapon's ocean appearance is a direct consequence of its construction. Its metal was repeatedly heated and cooled in seawater while the enchantment was being laid into it, and the finished trident never fully dries. When its magic is active, small streams of water crawl upward along the shaft regardless of gravity and gather around the tines before falling away. Anyone close enough can hear the muted sound of surf even if the nearest coastline is days away.

The trident was also designed to **awaken** through use. A newly made Ninth Current responds only to deliberate commands and can influence water through its stored charges. Once its bearer has repeatedly used it to protect others from dangerous water, the markings along the shaft begin spreading toward the grip. At that point, the weapon becomes Awakened and its bearer can instinctively feel the direction and strength of natural currents within several miles while holding it. The trident is not granting knowledge from nowhere. It is becoming better at doing what its makers intended, reading water as carefully as it commands it.

An Exalted Ninth Current is much rarer. The oldest Triton accounts describe tridents whose lines eventually cover the entire weapon, including the tines, until the metal resembles moving water trapped in the shape of a weapon. An Exalted trident can cast **Control Water once without spending charges**, regaining that use at dawn, and can maintain the effect even if the bearer releases the weapon. This allowed an experienced tidekeeper to stabilize a dangerous current and then physically enter the water to help whoever was trapped inside it.

The name comes from an old Triton teaching that divided a body of water into visible currents and hidden ones. Eight could be learned by observation, experience, and patience. The ninth was the current created by deliberate intervention, the path made when someone decided the water should move differently.

The weapon was never meant to dominate the sea. Its makers considered that impossible.

It was meant to convince one small part of it to cooperate.
