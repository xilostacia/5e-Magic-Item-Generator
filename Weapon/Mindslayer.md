Mindslayer is a Derro war staff built around the rib of an enormous purple worm. The rib was taken from a specimen old enough that much of the bone had begun to mineralize before it was ever worked. Its surface is a darkened ivory veined with deep violet deposits, polished only where hands naturally grip it. The natural curve of the rib was deliberately preserved, giving the staff a slight forward bend and an uneven balance that would be considered poor craftsmanship in almost any other weapon. Around the bone sit heavy bands of blackened metal, some fitted cleanly and others hammered into place wherever there was room. They were added across generations, and no attempt was ever made to make the different periods of work match.

The metal bands are what give Mindslayer much of its history. They were made from restraints taken from the Mind Flayers who had enslaved the Derro. Collars, shackles, chains, surgical braces, and pieces of equipment whose original purposes are no longer understood were broken apart and refitted around the purple worm rib. Illithid markings remain visible beneath the Derro writing because the makers refused to remove them. New inscriptions were cut directly through the old symbols. The result is not elegant, but elegance was never the intention. The Derro wanted the weapon to carry visible proof of what had been done to them, and they wanted every addition to the staff to be made from something taken back from their former masters.

The purple worm rib was chosen for more than its size or durability. The Derro had spent generations underground and understood the creatures of the deep earth intimately. A purple worm lives in a world of pressure, vibration, movement, and stone, and even its dead bones retain an uncanny ability to carry those things. Long before Mindslayer became a legendary weapon, Derro miners discovered that the rib could be pressed against cavern walls to feel movement through them. Footsteps, shifting doors, something dragging across stone, or a creature burrowing beyond sight all left different patterns in the bone. The first use of the rib may therefore have been as an alarm rather than a weapon. It gave the Derro something their masters had long denied them: warning.

From that discovery came the first form of Mindslayer. A grip was fitted to the rib, then reinforcement, then the first pieces of captured Illithid metal. The weapon changed whenever its wielders learned something new about killing Mind Flayers. It was never designed according to a single plan. One generation added resonance after discovering how violently certain vibrations affected their former masters. Another added protections against psychic intrusion. Another reconstructed fragments of Illithid telepathy and forced that stolen knowledge into the staff. Later wielders added ways of seeing through deception and recognizing movement through stone. Mindslayer became powerful because its makers never accepted that it was finished.

The staff's most infamous feature is the way it carries force through whatever it strikes. A blow from Mindslayer does not simply land against flesh or armor. The purple worm rib takes the impact, magnifies the vibration, and drives it inward. Victims describe the sensation as being struck from inside their own bones. Against Mind Flayers, that resonance changes noticeably. It seems to climb toward the skull and gather there, disrupting thought, balance, concentration, and the precise control upon which Illithid psionics depend. Old Derro records concerning the weapon are disturbingly clinical about this. They record what kinds of strikes caused an Illithid to release control of a slave, which frequencies broke concentration, and which left the creature unable to stand. The Derro had spent generations being studied. When they finally had the opportunity to study their captors in return, they were thorough.

Mindslayer was also built to resist what the Mind Flayers could do in return. The black iron bands around the rib tremble whenever powerful psychic influence presses against the wielder. The Derro came to believe that the restraints themselves remembered what they had once been used for. Whether that is literally true or simply how they understood the enchantment is impossible to prove, but the effect is consistent. Mindslayer fights intrusion. Attempts to dominate, compel, charm, or break the will of its bearer meet resistance from every layer of magic worked into the staff.

The weapon's telepathy is more uncomfortable because the Derro did not invent it. They stole it. The ability was reconstructed from Illithid devices, psychic techniques, and fragments of knowledge recovered after the revolt. One of the oldest surviving inscriptions on the staff was carved directly through a Mind Flayer glyph already present on one of the metal bands. The Derro words translate as, **You made us listen. Now you listen.** Through Mindslayer, its bearer can reach another mind without speaking, but the connection is never completely clean. Emotions sometimes return through it. Images flash without warning. A smell, a face, a remembered pain, or a word in an unknown language can bleed through from the other side.

Connecting to a Mind Flayer is considerably more dangerous. Illithids recognize the contact almost immediately, and some attempt to push back through it. Mindslayer can protect its wielder from being overwhelmed, but it cannot make that confrontation harmless. The Derro who built the ability knew this and accepted it. They had spent too much of their history unable to prevent something else from reaching into their minds. A connection dangerous to both sides was still preferable to one controlled entirely by the enemy.

The purple worm rib retained its older purpose even as the rest of the staff grew more complex. Underground, the bearer can still use it to read movement through continuous stone. To an experienced wielder, the surrounding tunnels stop feeling empty. Motion has direction, weight, and rhythm. Something walking upright feels different from something crawling. A heavy creature crossing a distant chamber produces a different pattern from a door being moved or a body dragged across the floor. Derro wielders eventually stopped describing the sensation as feeling tremors. Their surviving writings describe it as learning to hear the mountain.

Another generation added a different kind of perception after discovering that knowing where an enemy stood did not always mean knowing what it was. Mind Flayers and the servants around them used illusion, disguise, invisibility, transformation, and dominated intermediaries. Mindslayer was altered so that deception became increasingly difficult at close range. To its bearer, false forms sit badly against the world. Illusions separate from the things casting them. Invisible creatures cease to be truly absent. Shapechanged bodies carry the suggestion of what lies beneath. This sense was not part of the original staff, but like everything else on Mindslayer, it exists because someone once survived a failure and refused to let the next wielder suffer from the same weakness.

Only Warlocks can fully awaken the weapon's magic. This restriction developed from two separate bindings that originally required a spellcaster. When those bindings were layered together, they became more specific instead of redundant. The staff recognizes pact magic and rejects other forms of spellcasting. Derro explanations for this are philosophical rather than technical. Their makers had lived under creatures capable of turning a person's own mind against them, and they distrusted power that existed only inside the self. A Warlock's magic is shaped through a bond with something beyond the individual. Whatever the patron might be, the wielder already understands what it means to carry power that is not entirely their own and still remain themselves. One inscription near the grip summarizes the distinction in only a few words: **A master takes. A pact answers.**

Mindslayer is also dangerous to its bearer because the same resonance and psychic architecture that make it effective against Illithids are not perfectly contained. Powerful strikes send part of their force backward through the rib. Telepathic contact can carry thoughts in both directions. The staff protects its bearer from the enemy it was designed to kill, but it was never built to make that killing comfortable. The Derro accepted pain, psychic feedback, and physical strain as reasonable costs if the alternative was helplessness.

The staff can Awaken and Exalt because its makers intentionally left it unfinished. Empty portions of the metal bands remain unmarked, waiting for later hands. When Mindslayer is used to destroy powerful Illithids, break their control over others, or recover something useful from them, those spaces sometimes begin to fill. Some markings appear in the handwriting of the current wielder without ever being physically carved. Others use old Derro dialects that the bearer may not even understand. New seams form in the purple worm rib. Violet mineral lines brighten. Pieces of captured Illithid metal that had remained silent for generations suddenly begin to hum.

An Awakened Mindslayer becomes more aware of the enemy it was made to hunt. Illithid minds begin to stand out against the background pressure of other thoughts. The staff becomes more resistant to psychic interference, and the wielder gains greater control over the unwanted impressions that sometimes travel through its telepathy. Its ability to reveal hidden things also sharpens, as if the accumulated experience of previous wielders is still teaching the artifact what to distrust.

An Exalted Mindslayer becomes the closest thing the Derro ever created to a final answer to their former masters. When struck against the stone beneath it, the staff can release a vast resonant pulse through the surrounding ground. The effect is especially violent against Illithids, but its most important purpose is not killing them. It disrupts their control over other minds. Thralls stagger as psychic commands break apart. Dominated creatures experience brief moments of clarity. Mental links falter. The same weapon whose first purpose was to warn enslaved Derro that their masters were approaching eventually became capable of tearing those masters out of someone else's head.

For all the generations of psychic residue trapped inside it, Mindslayer is not sentient. That absence is deliberate. The staff has held the thoughts of countless creatures, touched the minds of its wielders, absorbed pieces of Illithid knowledge, and accumulated enough magical complexity that some form of consciousness might easily have emerged. Derro artificers repeatedly altered the weapon to prevent that from happening. They removed patterns that began behaving too much like memory, destroyed enchantments that responded without being invoked, and sealed away anything that seemed capable of developing preference or personality.

The reason appears repeatedly in the inscriptions.

The Derro had already spent generations with voices inside their heads.

They were never going to forge another one.

# Mindslayer

_Weapon (quarterstaff), legendary, requires attunement by a warlock_

Mindslayer is a magic quarterstaff that can also be used as a spellcasting focus for your warlock spells. Its properties change as the weapon Awakens and Exalts.

### Mindslayer, Unawakened

**Pact Weapon.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with Mindslayer. While using it as your spellcasting focus, you also gain a +2 bonus to your warlock spell attack rolls and warlock spell save DC.

**Resonant Strike.** When you hit a creature with Mindslayer, the attack deals an additional 2d6 thunder damage.

**Slayer of Illithids.** When the target is an Illithid, the attack deals an additional 2d8 thunder damage. For the purposes of this weapon, Illithids include mind flayers, ulitharids, elder brains, intellect devourers, mindwitnesses, and creatures the DM determines are directly created from or transformed by Illithids.

While holding Mindslayer, you have resistance to psychic damage dealt by Illithids and advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against their spells, psionic abilities, and other magical effects.

**Stolen Speech.** While attuned to Mindslayer, you can communicate telepathically with any creature you can see within 120 feet of you. You do not need to share a language with the creature for it to understand your thoughts, but a creature must understand at least one language to respond meaningfully.

**Wormsense.** While underground and touching a continuous surface of natural stone, earth, or worked stone directly connected to natural stone, you have tremorsense out to 120 feet. Mindslayer must be on your person and in contact with you for this sense to function.

**Unmasked Sight.** While holding Mindslayer, you have truesight out to 30 feet.

**The Open Mind.** Mindslayer's stolen telepathy works in both directions. Whenever you establish telepathic contact with an Illithid, that creature immediately knows your direction and approximate distance from it while you remain within 120 feet. The first time during that connection that either you or the Illithid deals psychic damage to the other, you both take psychic damage equal to your proficiency bonus. This damage cannot be reduced or prevented.

Mindslayer's creators considered this an acceptable flaw. If they could reach an Illithid's mind, they were willing to let it reach back.

### Awakening Mindslayer

Mindslayer Awakens when its bearer accomplishes a significant act against Illithid domination. Suitable events include slaying a powerful Illithid, destroying an Illithid colony, breaking the control of an elder brain, freeing a large number of thralls, or recovering important knowledge from an Illithid stronghold. The DM determines when the weapon Awakens.

When Mindslayer Awakens, it gains the following properties.

**Greater Resonance.** Mindslayer's bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, warlock spell attack rolls, and warlock spell save DC becomes +3. Its Resonant Strike damage becomes 3d6 thunder damage, and its additional damage against Illithids becomes 3d8 thunder damage.

**Unmasked Sight.** Your truesight increases to 60 feet.

**Know the Master.** While underground, you are aware whenever an Illithid is within 1,000 feet of you. You know the direction of the creature, but not its exact distance or identity. Effects that prevent divination do not block this sense because Mindslayer detects the psychic and physical disturbances associated with Illithids rather than locating them through divination magic.

**Sever the Chain.** When a creature you can see within 60 feet fails a saving throw against being charmed, dominated, or otherwise mentally controlled by an Illithid, you can use your reaction to force that creature to immediately repeat the saving throw with advantage. On a success, the effect ends on that creature.

Once you use this property, you cannot use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Exalting Mindslayer

Mindslayer Exalts only after it has become Awakened and its bearer accomplishes an act that permanently destroys a major source of Illithid power. Defeating an elder brain, ending an Illithid dominion, destroying a powerful psionic artifact, or breaking an established system of Illithid enslavement are suitable events. The DM determines when the weapon Exalts.

When Mindslayer Exalts, it gains the following properties.

**Perfected Resonance.** Resonant Strike deals 4d6 thunder damage. Against Illithids, Mindslayer deals an additional 4d8 thunder damage instead of 3d8.

**Perfected Sight.** Your truesight increases to 120 feet.

**Unbroken Mind.** You cannot be charmed or frightened by Illithids, and Illithids cannot read your thoughts unless you willingly allow them to do so. If an Illithid attempts to dominate or otherwise directly control your actions, you automatically know that the attempt was made even if the effect would normally conceal itself.

**Break the Hive.** As an action, you can strike Mindslayer against stone and unleash the full resonance of the purple worm rib. Each Illithid of your choice within 120 feet must make a Constitution saving throw against your warlock spell save DC. On a failed save, a creature takes 8d8 thunder damage and 8d8 psychic damage and is stunned until the end of your next turn. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and is not stunned.

At the same time, each creature of your choice within 120 feet that is charmed, dominated, possessed, or otherwise mentally controlled by an Illithid can immediately repeat any saving throw normally permitted by that effect, with advantage. If the effect normally allows no further saving throw, the creature instead makes a Wisdom saving throw against the Illithid's save DC, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Once Mindslayer uses Break the Hive, it cannot do so again until the next dawn.

**No Voice Within.** Mindslayer is not sentient and cannot become sentient through ordinary magical means. Attempts to awaken a consciousness within the weapon automatically fail unless the magic involved is powerful enough to alter a legendary magic item directly.
